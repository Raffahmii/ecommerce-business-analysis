
## Konsep paling kuat: **E-commerce Business Performance & Customer Behavior Analysis**

Project ini cocok banget buat portfolio karena:
- ada **sales**
- ada **customer behavior**
- ada **product performance**
- ada **region / payment / discount**
- bisa dibuat **dashboard profesional**
- bisa dianalisis dari SQL sampai Tableau

### Dataset yang dicari
Cari dataset yang punya kolom seperti:
- order_id
- order_date
- customer_id
- product_category
- product_name
- sales / revenue
- quantity
- discount
- profit
- region / city
- payment method
- customer segment
- shipping mode
- returned / cancelled order

### Keyword buat cari data
Coba cari di Kaggle / Google / UCI pakai keyword ini:
- `ecommerce sales dataset`
- `online retail dataset`
- `customer behavior dataset`
- `retail transaction dataset`
- `sales and profit dataset`
- `order level dataset`
- `ecommerce customer segmentation dataset`
- `retail analytics dataset`

Kalau mau yang lebih “business analyst banget”, cari yang ada:
- customer id
- order date
- category
- sales
- profit
- discount
- region

# Struktur analisis yang gua saranin

## 1. SQL
Pakai SQL buat:
- cek data quality
- total revenue
- total orders
- average order value
- sales by category
- sales by region
- sales by month
- top products
- discount analysis
- customer segment analysis

Contoh output SQL yang bagus:
- monthly trend
- top 10 products
- bottom 10 products
- revenue per region
- revenue per payment method
- average profit margin by category

---

## 2. Python / Pandas / NumPy
Pakai Python buat:
- cleaning data
- missing value check
- duplicate check
- data type fixing
- feature engineering
- RFM analysis
- cohort analysis sederhana
- correlation analysis
- business summary

Kalau mau kelihatan profesional:
- bikin kolom `month`
- bikin `revenue_per_order`
- bikin `profit_margin`
- bikin `customer_segment`
- bikin `order_frequency`
- bikin `repeat_customer_flag`

Kalau datasetnya cukup lengkap, lu bisa bikin:
- **RFM segmentation**
- **top customer analysis**
- **repeat purchase analysis**

---

## 3. Excel
Jangan cuma dipakai biasa. Pakai Excel buat:
- pivot table
- pivot chart
- dashboard ringkas
- slicer
- KPI summary

Excel cocok buat nunjukin lu masih kuat di business reporting.  
Misalnya:
- sales by month
- sales by category
- region performance
- discount vs sales
- top customer

Kalau mau, Excel dashboard ini bisa jadi versi “quick business report”.

---

## 4. Tableau
Ini yang jadi tampilan utama portfolio.

Dashboard Tableau bisa isi:
- KPI cards
- sales trend
- category performance
- regional performance
- customer segment
- discount analysis
- top products
- profit analysis

Kalau dataset bagus, Tableau lu bisa dibuat 2 halaman:
1. **Executive Overview**
2. **Customer & Product Insights**

# Analisis kompleks yang bisa lu masukin

Ini yang bikin project lu naik level.

## A. Profitability Analysis
- kategori paling untung

- produk yang margin-nya kecil

## B. Customer Segmentation
- high-value customer
- low-frequency customer
- repeat customer
- customer baru vs lama

## C. Discount Effect
- diskon tinggi naikkan sales atau malah turunkan profit?
- diskon mana yang paling efektif?

## D. Regional Performance
- region yang paling kuat
- region yang butuh promo
- distribusi sales per wilayah

## E. Product Analysis
- top product
- slow moving product
- produk yang sering dibeli bareng kalau datanya ada

## F. Trend Analysis
- month-over-month growth
- seasonality
- peak season
- penurunan performa
