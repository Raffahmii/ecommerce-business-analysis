\# 🛒 E-commerce Business Performance \& Customer Segmentation Analysis



An end-to-end \*\*Data Analytics \& Business Intelligence project\*\* focused on analyzing e-commerce transaction data to discover business insights, customer behavior patterns, and performance opportunities.



> 🚧 \*\*Project Status: In Progress\*\*

>

> This project is currently under development. The analysis workflow, data processing, dashboards, and business recommendations will be continuously improved.



\---



\# 📌 Project Overview



This project aims to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights using:



\- Data Cleaning

\- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

\- SQL Business Analysis

\- Excel Reporting

\- Tableau Dashboard Development

\- Business Insight Generation



The main objective is to understand:



\- Sales performance

\- Product performance

\- Customer purchasing behavior

\- Regional trends

\- Discount effectiveness

\- Business growth opportunities



\---



\# 🎯 Business Objective



An e-commerce company wants to better understand its sales performance and customer behavior.



This analysis will answer important business questions such as:



\### Sales Performance

\- What products generate the highest revenue?

\- How does revenue change over time?

\- Which categories contribute the most sales?



\### Customer Behavior

\- Who are the most valuable customers?

\- How can customers be segmented based on purchasing behavior?

\- What factors influence repeat purchases?



\### Business Optimization

\- Do discounts increase sales performance?

\- Which regions have the highest potential?

\- Which products need improvement?
